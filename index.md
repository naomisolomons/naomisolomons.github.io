My interests are, broadly, how quantum information differs from the classical world and how this can be harnessed for a useful advantage. I have worked on different topics in quantum information, including quantum communication and metrology, quantum advantage and complexity theory, quantum optics, and measurement based quantum computing. During my PhD, I studied Gaussian boson sampling, and during my first postdoc I worked on distributed quantum sensing. I now work on topics related to nonlocality, shallow circuit complexity, cryptography, and resource theories. I am also currently interested in contextuality and randomness.

I grew up in Essex, in the UK, and did my undergrad at Durham University, then joined the Quantum Engineering CDT at the University of Bristol (now the [QIST CDT](https://www.qist-cdt.info/)). During my PhD I did an internship with [Riverlane](https://www.riverlane.com/) and worked part-time for [Duality Quantum Photonics](https://www.dualityqp.com/). I then spent two years in Paris in the [LIP6 QI group](https://qi.lip6.fr/), and I am now a postdoc at the [University of Calgary](https://iqst.ucalgary.ca/index.php). 

To see a list of my papers, you can look at my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=1DMjizQAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). You can download my thesis [here](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/applications-of-gaussian-boson-sampling-in-graph-theory/). When I was writing my thesis, I also wrote a considerably longer 'annotated copy'. This is not peer-reviewed (and mostly not read by anyone other than myself), but contains extra notes that I wrote explaining concepts in more detail or adding extra details I found interesting. You can download it [here](./memoirthesis.pdf). I hope someone else finds it useful!

I am interested in outreach and EDI. As a student I wrote several articles for [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/author/naomi-solomons/), and organised 2 years of [Quantum In The Summer](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/qet-labs/outreach/quantum-summer/). I was a volunteer with Serve The City Paris, which you can read about [here](https://www.servethecity.paris/2026/07/22/volunteer-spotlight-naomi/), and I am currently a mentor with [The Mullany Fund](https://themullanyfund.org/).
